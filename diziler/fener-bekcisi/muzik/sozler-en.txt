Fener Bekcisi — Theme Song (English)
Suno style: sea shanty folk ballad, warm male vocals, accordion, solo violin, ocean waves, gentle drums, nostalgic and warm, English lyrics
Trailer music style: maritime mystery trailer, ocean waves ambience, foghorn-like low brass, solo violin, sea shanty motif, slow epic build, haunting but warm

Whatever the waves may say
